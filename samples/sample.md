# Sample: the two hosts

COHOST: So, how do you actually teach a computer what a cat looks like?

HOST: Honestly? The same way you'd teach a kid. You don't hand them a rulebook. You just point. Cat. Cat. Not a cat.

COHOST: Okay, fair. But if it's that simple, why did it only start working around 2012?

HOST: Because it turns out you need millions of examples, and chips fast enough to learn from all of them.

COHOST: Huh. So the idea was there all along, just waiting for the hardware.

HOST: Exactly. And that's kind of the story of this whole series.
